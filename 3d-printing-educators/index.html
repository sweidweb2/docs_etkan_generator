ETKAN 3D Printing for Educators 01

Program Overview

From Idea to Classroom

This training program is designed with a practical, hands-on approach to equip teachers with the essential skills needed to integrate 3D printing into their classrooms.

The program focuses on enabling educators to independently design, prepare, and produce educational resources using simple, accessible tools that require no prior engineering or CAD experience. By the end of the training, participants will have the confidence to create customized teaching aids and classroom models that enhance student engagement and support interactive learning.

Program Objectives

Upon successful completion, participants will be able to:

  • Understand the fundamentals of 3D printing technology.
  • Safely operate the Bambu Lab A1 3D printer.
  • Prepare models for printing using Bambu Studio.
  • Design simple educational models using Tinkercad.
  • Export designs in STL format.
  • Configure print settings according to project requirements.
  • Produce high-quality prints and troubleshoot common printing issues.

Platform & Equipment

Why These Tools

Why Tinkercad?

Ideally suited for educators new to 3D design — teachers focus on transforming ideas into educational resources instead of learning complex engineering software.

  • Completely free platform
  • No software installation required
  • Browser-based from any computer
  • Intuitive beginner interface
  • Short learning curve — productive within hours
  • Essential tools for classroom-ready models
Why Bambu Lab A1?

Selected for the balance of simplicity, reliability, and performance in educational environments.

  • Easy operation
  • Fast, high-quality printing
  • Minimal maintenance
  • Automatic calibration that reduces user errors
  • Ideal for first-time users and institutions

Training Modules

Five Modules, One Path

The program follows a highly practical, project-based learning approach that emphasizes hands-on experience over theory, structured across five progressive modules.

01
3D Design with Tinkercad
  • Navigate the Tinkercad workspace
  • Create basic 3D objects
  • Apply accurate dimensions
  • Align and organize objects
  • Combine and subtract shapes
  • Add 3D text
  • Design simple educational models for classroom use
02
Preparing Models for Printing
  • Exporting STL files
  • Using Bambu Studio
  • Selecting appropriate print settings
  • Sending models to the printer
  • Monitoring the printing process
03
Introduction to 3D Printing
  • What is 3D printing?
  • Educational applications of 3D printing
  • Types of printing materials (filaments)
  • Components of a 3D printer
  • Safety procedures and best practices
04
Operating the Bambu Lab A1
  • Printer overview
  • Loading filament
  • Automatic calibration
  • Starting a print
  • Removing finished models safely
  • Routine maintenance and care
05
Practical Classroom Project

Each participant will design and print an educational model related to their teaching subject. Possible examples include:

  • Geometric models
  • Science teaching aids
  • Classroom nameplates
  • Tool holders and organizers
  • Educational demonstrations
  • Subject-specific learning resources

Training Methodology

The program follows a highly practical, project-based learning approach that emphasizes hands-on experience over theory. Training includes:

  • Concise theoretical introductions
  • Live demonstrations
  • Guided practical exercises
  • Individual hands-on practice with the equipment
  • Real classroom-oriented design and printing projects

Expected Learning Outcomes

By the end of the program, every participant will be able to:

  • Design a simple 3D model independently.
  • Prepare it for printing using Bambu Studio.
  • Print it successfully on the Bambu Lab A1.
  • Apply these skills to create customized educational resources for their own classrooms.
