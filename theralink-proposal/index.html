The digital workspace for modern physiotherapy practices.

TheraLink brings patient records, appointments, sessions, exercises, and practice admin into one place — so therapists spend less time chasing paperwork and more time with patients.

The problem

Most physiotherapy practices still run on scattered tools. Day to day, that usually means:

  • Patient information and session history scattered across notebooks, chats, and files
  • No clear way for patients to stay connected with their care outside the clinic
  • Manual appointment follow-ups — easy to miss, hard to keep consistent
  • Assessments done on paper, with little visibility into progress over time
  • No clear view of sessions completed, missed, canceled, or peak clinic hours
  • Payments and practice finances tracked separately from clinical work

What TheraLink provides

One workspace built around how physiotherapy practices actually run — including:

Patient portal Patients stay connected to their care from outside the clinic
WhatsApp reminders Automatic appointment reminders that reduce missed visits
Practice analytics Sessions, missed and canceled visits, peak hours, and more
Assessment templates Dynamic clinical forms with graphs to track progress
Finance & accounting Payments and practice finances in the same workspace
Cases, sessions & scheduling Records, treatments, and appointments organized together

Spend less time managing the practice, and more time focusing on your patients.
