01

A two-person studio that ships the work itself

TwoGate Digital is a founder-led web studio based in Beirut and working remotely. We design and build corporate websites, SaaS products and the content platforms that teams run themselves — end to end, without a large agency layer in between.

Both founders write production code. There is no account-manager layer, no design-to-development handoff, and no junior bench. Clients work directly with the people who ship the site.

The work spans education platforms, B2B marketing sites, food-tech directories, agency sites and live SaaS products with paying customers. The through-line is the same: clear information architecture, a CMS the client can actually operate, and a clean handover.

TwoGate Digital Section 03 · What we build
03

Corporate sites, product UI, and the CMS behind them

We are not a media agency and we are not a twenty-person production house. The studio is built to take a public website from a frozen sitemap to a live, editable platform — including the admin the client’s team will actually use.

01

Corporate & marketing sites

Brand-faithful public sites for B2B and institutional clients: catalogues, program pages, case studies, multi-category structure and conversion-led layouts.

02

Custom admin & CMS

Next.js admin panels on a real database: content types, CRUD, roles, forms and the business logic a brief actually describes — plus editor training at handover.

03

SaaS product interfaces

Dashboards, calendars, approvals and analytics for live products with paying customers. The same discipline we apply to a corporate site: clear IA, fast pages, no leftover prototype.

04

Portfolio & content archives

Filterable portfolios, insights archives, course/program structures and listing directories — the pattern ASIP’s Portfolio and Insights sections need.

05

Launch & handover

QA on mainstream browsers, staging for UAT, go-live, a written handover pack and a warranty window. The client’s editors should be able to run the site without us in the room.

06

Stack we typically ship

Next.js on Vercel, Supabase (PostgreSQL + storage), custom admin rather than a boxed CMS. We adapt hosting, database or CMS pattern if the client prefers something else.
