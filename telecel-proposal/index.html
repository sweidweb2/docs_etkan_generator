Delivery approach

Build runs in sequence: wireframes, then public frontend only, then the admin panel, then database wiring, then launch. No Figma — only simple general wireframes.

Proposed stack — our preference

The stack below reflects the recommended approach based on delivery speed, maintainability and this freelancer's experience. We are open to Telecel's input: if you prefer a different hosting provider, database, CMS pattern or framework, we can adapt the architecture and revise third-party costs accordingly before kick-off.

FrontendNext.js
Hosting / CDNVercel Pro
DatabaseSupabase PostgreSQL
StorageSupabase Storage
CMSCustom Next.js admin
DomainExisting Telecel domain
EmailZoho Mail (optional)
LoggingApplication logging
Phase 01

Kick-off and wireframes

Confirm scope, sitemap and content inventory against the RFQ's core page list. Simple general wireframes (not Figma). Product owner named and page list frozen.

Phase 02

Frontend development

Build the public site only in Next.js: page templates, responsive layout, Telecel brand guidelines and portfolio/filter structure. Static templates — not yet connected to the database.

Phase 03

Admin panel — CRUD and logic

Custom Next.js admin for Telecel: content types, CRUD flows, roles, forms and the business logic described in the brief — on Supabase and PostgreSQL.

Phase 04

Connect public pages to the database

Wire the public frontend to live data from Supabase. Populate agreed pages from Telecel-supplied copy and media. SEO fundamentals: meta titles and descriptions, Open Graph tags, sitemap and baseline on-page structure. Staging reflects the full end-to-end flow.

Phase 05

Testing, training, launch and handover

QA across mainstream browsers and priority devices, editor training, go-live on Vercel, and a 30-day warranty window.

Warranty terms

For 30 calendar days after go-live (matching the RFQ warranty period), defects in the delivered templates and platform are corrected at no extra fee.   • Beirut business hours; material defects targeted within two working days.
