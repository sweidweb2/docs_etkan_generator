Workshop Proposal:
Get Familiar with 3D Printers

Presented by ETKAN
Audience
University engineering students, all majors
Duration
2 hours
Group size
15–20 participants, practice in groups of 4

Purpose

This workshop introduces engineering students to 3D printing and shows them how it fits into modern manufacturing. By the end, participants will understand how a 3D printer works, what materials it uses, and how a digital model becomes a physical part.

Participants leave able to prepare and start a real print, whatever their major.

Agenda

  1. The future of manufacturing
  2. Introduction to materials
  3. The idea of controlling CNC machines
  4. Main 3D printing parameters
  5. Practice
Note
Please bring your laptop and install Bambu Studio before the workshop.
