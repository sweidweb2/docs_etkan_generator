01

Project intent

Build a Civil Engineering Marketplace and Project Management Platform for the Lebanese construction market first: one centralized ecosystem connecting project managers, workers, equipment and machinery providers, material suppliers, and customers.

The platform is a web application and mobile applications, on a shared backend and database. The aim is to reduce fragmentation — making it easier to find, compare, hire, rent, purchase, and manage construction-related resources, with verification and admin moderation so the market can trust what it sees.

Market
Lebanon first, construction ecosystem
Surfaces

Outcome
Trusted place to organize work
02

Who uses the platform

Project Managers

Create and manage construction projects: information and budgets, assign workers, rent machinery and equipment, manage materials, track expenses and overall financial performance. Dedicated project workspace for resources, payments, and simplified project accounting.

Providers & companies

Verified profiles. Publish machinery, trucks, equipment, and construction materials for rental or sale — pricing, specifications, availability, images, and related information.

Workers

Professional profiles: skills, hourly or custom rates, experience, CVs, previous projects, portfolios, certifications. Profile changes go through administrator approval. Verification to raise trust.

Customers / guests

Browse and discover workers, equipment, and materials without necessarily creating an account. Registered customers keep history and activity. Purchase materials and use platform services.
