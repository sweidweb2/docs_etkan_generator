The project is a comprehensive **Civil Engineering Marketplace and Project Management Platform** designed initially for the Lebanese construction market, connecting project managers, workers, equipment and machinery providers, material suppliers, and customers within one centralized ecosystem. The platform will consist of a web application and mobile applications, supported by a shared backend and database. **Project Managers** will be able to create and manage construction projects, define project information and budgets, assign workers, rent machinery and equipment, manage required materials, and track project expenses and overall financial performance. **Providers and companies** will be able to create verified profiles and publish their available machinery, trucks, equipment, and construction materials for rental or sale, including pricing, specifications, availability, images, and other relevant information. **Workers** will be able to create professional profiles containing their skills, hourly or customized rates, experience, CVs, previous projects, portfolios, certifications, and other professional information, with profile changes subject to administrator approval. **Customers/Guests** will be able to browse and discover workers, equipment, and construction materials without necessarily creating an account, while registered customers can maintain their history and activity. The platform will also include a structured **construction materials directory**, allowing users to understand materials, their technical information, units, specifications, suppliers, and relevant market information, helping reduce misinformation and unfair pricing. Users will be able to purchase materials and use the platform's available services, while workers, materials, providers, and other marketplace entities can receive ratings and reviews subject to administrator moderation and approval. The system will support verification of workers and providers to increase trust and reliability within the construction ecosystem. An extensive **Admin Dashboard** will provide centralized control over users, verification, workers, providers, projects, equipment, rentals, materials, orders, transactions, reviews, reports, documents, and system settings. Project Managers will also have a dedicated project workspace for managing project information, assigned resources, workers, equipment, materials, expenses, payments, and simplified project accounting. The platform will support online transactions and is designed to later incorporate a subscription-based model for Project Managers. The overall objective is to create a trusted digital infrastructure for the construction industry where resources, professionals, materials, equipment, projects, and financial activities can be organized and managed in one platform, reducing fragmentation and making it easier to find, compare, hire, rent, purchase, and manage construction-related resources.
