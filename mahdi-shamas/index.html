Athlete Pod

Light-based reaction training for physio therapy and sport medicine

The pods light up in patterns. The patient taps them as fast and as accurately as they can. Every tap is saved and sent to the app right away.

The idea came from products like BlazePod. We then built our own from scratch — the circuit, the case, the software inside the pod, and the app — so clinics get a full training tool with clear session data.

Etkan Engineering Teams Athlete Pod · Overview 03

How It Works

Who it is for

Physio therapists, physio therapy and sport medicine centers, sports clinics, hospitals, and private clinics that need a way to measure reaction training.

What it does

  • Lights up in patterns across several pods
  • Records reaction time, accuracy, taps, and session length
  • Lets the therapist run the session from a cross platform mobile app
  • Ready-made exercises for common physio and sport medicine goals
  • Build your own exercises — pods, timing, order, and difficulty
  • Playlists that run warmup → training → cool-down on their own
  • Patient history, progress graphs, and a recovery score over time

Main features

CharacteristicsSends data, lights up, and feels taps
Cross platform mobile appSet up, run sessions, and see history on phone or tablet
Custom exercisesControl pods, timing, order, and difficulty
Progress trackingSee trends, compare sessions, and get a recovery score
Built as one systemCircuit, case, software, and app made together

App, Exercises & Data

The app is a cross platform mobile app, so it works on phone and tablet. Therapists use it to set up exercises, watch live sessions, and check patient history. The screens are simple, so someone can start quickly.

Exercises

  • Ready-made exercises for common physio and sport medicine goals
  • Easy, medium, and hard levels
  • Custom exercises built by the therapist
  • Playlists that link exercises into a full session

What each session records

Per tap
Reaction time & accuracy
Per session
Total taps & duration

Over time, this becomes a full history for each patient. The app turns that into graphs and a recovery score — so it is clear if someone is getting better, staying the same, or needs a new plan. Therapists can look at reaction times over weeks and compare sessions side by side.

Strength is only one part. Athlete Pod trains how fast the brain and body react, and it measures that in real sessions.

EMDR Device

A kit that helps therapists run EMDR Device sessions

The EMDR Device is used in a therapy that helps people work through hard memories. During a session, the patient follows a left–right signal with their eyes, hears sounds in each ear, or feels pulses in each hand.

This kit does that with hardware, so the therapist does not have to move a hand left and right the whole time. It has a light bar for the eyes to follow, headphones for sound, and handheld buzzers — all controlled from a tablet or mobile.

Etkan Engineering Teams EMDR Device · Overview 08

How EMDR Device Stimulation Works

Who it is for

Therapists trained in EMDR Device, psychologists, and mental-health clinics that need a steady left–right signal during in-person sessions.

The three ways it works

  • Eyes — a light moves left–right on the bar; the patient follows it
  • Sound — tones switch from one ear to the other in the headphones
  • Touch — handheld buzzers pulse left and right
  • Each way can run alone, or they can run together

Main features

Light barA LED bar on a stand for the eyes to follow
Speed & strengthThe therapist can change speed, brightness, and strength
SoundHeadphones for left–right tones
Hand buzzersHandheld buzzers for left–right pulses
Session controlTablet or mobile to start, pause, and change settings
Easier for the therapistNo need to wave a hand left and right the whole session
Etkan Engineering Teams EMDR Device · How It Works 09

EMDR Device — System Overview

In a session, the therapist picks the type of signal and the settings, then starts the left–right pattern. The hardware does the repeating left–right work, so the therapist can stay focused on the patient.

Hardware
  • LED light bar + stand
  • Over-ear headphones
  • Pair of hand buzzers
  • Controller (tablet or mobile)
Session control
  • Start / pause the signal
  • Change speed, brightness, and volume
  • Pick eyes, sound, touch, or all together
  • Change settings to what the patient can handle
